Slideshows of your Plex photos, on your phone or your TV

Updated November 27, 2026

Start a slideshow from any folder, any year or your whole Plex photo library. Choose 2, 4, 6 or 10 seconds a slide, with shuffle and loop. With Screen Mirroring on, or over an HDMI adapter, Phlix Pro puts just the photos on your TV while your iPhone shows the controls.

  • A folder, a year or everything. A folder's slideshow also plays the folders inside it.
  • Your pace. 2, 4, 6 or 10 seconds a slide, with shuffle and loop.
  • On your TV. With Pro, the TV shows just the photos, full screen.

Set it up your way

Tap Slideshow in a folder or on the timeline. Choose how long each photo stays, from 2 to 10 seconds, and switch Shuffle, Loop and Include Videos on or off.

Folders inside folders

A folder's slideshow also plays the folders inside it, however many levels deep. Switch off Include Subfolders to play just that one folder.

On your TV

Turn on Screen Mirroring from Control Center, or plug in an HDMI adapter. With Phlix Pro, the TV shows just the photos, full screen, while your iPhone or iPad shows the controls. Without Pro, the TV mirrors your screen.

Screen Mirroring needs an Apple TV or a TV that supports AirPlay. An HDMI adapter works with any TV that has an HDMI input.

Questions

Can I show a Plex photo slideshow on my TV?

Yes. Turn on Screen Mirroring from Control Center, or use an HDMI adapter. With Phlix Pro, the TV shows just the photos, full screen, while your iPhone or iPad shows the controls. Without Pro, the TV mirrors your screen.

Can a slideshow include videos?

Yes. Switch on Include Videos in the slideshow's options.

Does a folder's slideshow include the folders inside it?

Yes, however many levels deep. Switch off Include Subfolders to play just that folder.

How long does each photo stay on screen?

2, 4, 6 or 10 seconds, your choice. Shuffle and Loop are in the same options.
