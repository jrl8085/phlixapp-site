What Phlix does

Updated November 27, 2026

Phlix is an independent iPhone and iPad app that works with Plex Media Server. It backs up your camera roll to your own server, saves photos and videos from your server to your iPhone, and shows your whole photo library on a date timeline, with slideshows and search. Free to browse, with no ads.

Camera Upload Beta Back up the photos and videos on your iPhone or iPad to your own Plex server, on your home Wi-Fi only. How it works and setup → Save to Photos Download photos and videos from your Plex server to the Photos app on your iPhone, or straight into an album. Learn more → Timeline Every photo on your server by date, with a year scrubber, built for libraries of 100,000 photos and more. Learn more → Slideshows Slideshows of a folder, a year or the whole library, on your iPhone or on your TV. Learn more → Browse and Search The folders on your server as you organized them, search by title, tag and collection, and favorites. Learn more →

Also in Phlix

  • Easy on your data plan. On cellular and in Low Data Mode, Phlix stops loading ahead of you. On Wi-Fi it loads at full quality.
  • iPhone, iPad and Mac. On iPad, a sidebar lists your libraries. A Mac with Apple silicon can run the iPad version from the App Store. VoiceOver works throughout.
  • In your language. English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Polish, Japanese, Korean and Chinese, Simplified and Traditional.
  • No Phlix account. You sign in on Plex's own sign-in page, so Phlix never sees your password. Phlix never changes or deletes what is on your server.
