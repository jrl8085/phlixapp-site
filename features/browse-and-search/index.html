Feature · Browse and Search

Your Plex folders, search and favorites

Updated November 27, 2026

Phlix shows the folders on your Plex server exactly as you organized them, and searches photo and video titles, tags and collections across your library. Mark the keepers with a heart: favorites are saved on your device.

  • The same folders as your server. In the same order, with a path at the top.
  • Search. Titles, tags and collections, across the library.
  • Your server or a shared one. Switch between its photo libraries.

The folders you organized

Phlix follows the folders on your server, in the same order, with the path at the top so you always know where you are. A folder's slideshow is one tap away.

Search the whole library

Search photo and video titles, tags and collections, and open any result full screen.

Favorites

Tap the heart on a photo or video to keep it close. Favorites are saved on your device and have their own tab.

Full screen, photos and clips

Swipe through a day or a folder, pinch to zoom, and play video clips in a player that remembers where you stopped. HEIC, RAW and TIFF photos are converted by your Plex server; if it cannot convert one, Phlix falls back to another copy the server can send.

Your server, or one shared with you

Use your own Plex Media Server or one shared with you, and switch between its photo libraries. On iPad, a sidebar lists your libraries. A Mac with Apple silicon can run the iPad version from the App Store.

Phlix shows photo libraries only: it does not play your movie, TV or music libraries.

Questions

Does Phlix work with a Plex server shared with me?

Yes. Use your own Plex Media Server or one shared with you, and switch between its photo libraries.

Does Phlix play movies or music from Plex?

No. Phlix shows photo libraries only.

Can I use Phlix on an iPad or a Mac?

Yes. On iPad, a sidebar lists your libraries. A Mac with Apple silicon can run the iPad version from the App Store.

Does Phlix change anything on my server?

No. Phlix never changes or deletes what is on your server. Camera Upload (Beta) only adds new files, in the folder you choose for it.
