Feature · Timeline

Every photo on your Plex server, by date

Updated November 27, 2026

Phlix shows the photos and videos in your Plex photo library on one timeline, sorted by date, with the years down the side: tap 1987 and you are there. It is built for libraries of 100,000 photos and more.

  • A year scrubber. Tap a year to jump straight to it.
  • Built for big libraries. Phlix loads a page at a time instead of everything at once.
  • Free. Browsing and the timeline are free, with no ads.

Jump to any year

The years run down the side of the timeline. Tap one and the timeline jumps there, so decades of photos are a tap apart. It works the way the Photos app does, with the photos on your own server.

Built for 100,000 photos and more

Phlix loads a page at a time instead of your whole library at once. The first screen appears in seconds, and the year index fills in behind it, even in a very large library.

On cellular data and in Low Data Mode, Phlix stops loading ahead of you. On Wi-Fi it loads at full quality.

Folders or the timeline

Phlix also shows your library the way your server has it: the same folders, in the same order. One tap switches between the folders and the timeline.

Open, zoom, play

Tap a photo to see it full screen, pinch to zoom, and swipe to the next one. Videos play in a player that remembers where you stopped.

Questions

Can I see my Plex photos by date on an iPhone?

Yes. Phlix shows the photos in your Plex photo library on a date timeline with a year scrubber, on iPhone and iPad. Browsing is free.

Does it work with a very large library?

Yes. Phlix is built for libraries of 100,000 photos and more. It loads a page at a time, so the first screen appears in seconds.

Can I still browse by folder?

Yes. One tap switches between the timeline and the folders on your server, in the same order as on the server.

Does Phlix change my library?

No. Phlix never changes or deletes what is on your server.
