Feature · Save to Photos

Save photos and videos from your Plex server to your iPhone

Updated November 27, 2026

Phlix downloads the photos and videos on your Plex Media Server into the Photos app on your iPhone or iPad, or straight into an album you choose. Once saved, they stay on your device whether or not your server is reachable.

  • Photos and videos. Open one and tap ••• to save it, or long-press it in a folder or on the timeline.
  • Into an album, if you like. Choose an album once, and every save goes into it.
  • 25 saves free. Phlix Pro saves without a limit.

Save a photo or a video

Open a photo or video in Phlix and tap •••. The menu has three ways to keep it:

  • Save to Photos saves it now. Phlix then says it is saved, and how many free saves you have left.
  • Download saves it to Photos in the background, so you can keep browsing.
  • Save to Album… chooses the album your saves go into.

Straight into an album

Choose an album once, under Settings › Storage › Save to Album, or with Save to Album… on any photo. Every photo you save from Phlix then goes into it. Pick one of your albums or make a new one, and choose No Album to stop.

Albums need full access to your photo library, so choosing one asks iOS for it.

The original file, when your server sends it

Phlix asks your Plex server for the original file, and when the server sends it, that is what you get. When it cannot, Phlix saves the copy your server converts, which may be smaller. A file in a format the Photos app cannot store can go to the Files app instead.

Why save a photo to your iPhone

  • To share it. Messages, mail and other apps share from your photo library, and cannot reach your Plex server.
  • To have it with you. A saved photo stays on your iPhone when your server is off or out of reach.
  • To edit it. The Photos app's editing tools work on saved photos.

How to save a photo from your Plex server to your iPhone

  1. Download Phlix free from the App Store and sign in with your Plex account, on Plex's own sign-in page.
  2. Open a photo or video from your server, in a folder or on the timeline.
  3. Tap ••• and choose Save to Photos.
  4. Find it in the Photos app with your other photos, or in the album you chose.

Questions

Can I download photos from my Plex server to my iPhone?

Yes. Phlix, an iPhone and iPad app that works with Plex Media Server, saves the photos and videos on your server into the Photos app, or into an album you choose. The first 25 saves are free, and Phlix Pro saves without a limit.

Can I save videos too?

Yes. Videos save to the Photos app the same way as photos.

Does Phlix save the original file?

When your Plex server sends the original, yes. When it cannot, Phlix saves the copy your server converts, which may be smaller. A file in a format the Photos app cannot store can be saved to the Files app instead.

Where do saved photos go?

Into the Photos app on your iPhone or iPad, with your other photos, or into the album you chose under Settings, Storage, Save to Album. They stay there if you delete Phlix.

Will Camera Upload send saved photos back to my server?

No. Since version 1.0.21, Phlix keeps a list of the photos it saves from your server, and Camera Upload on that device does not upload them back.
