Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to use Phlix?

A Plex account (free or Plex Pass) and a Plex Media Server with at least one photo library. Your device must be running iOS 17 or later.

How does the free trial work?

You get 14 days of full access from the first time you sign in. After the trial, a one-time purchase of $4.99 unlocks the app permanently. No subscription required.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Phlix, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchase." Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

I don't see any photo libraries.

Phlix only displays photo-type libraries from your Plex server. Make sure your Plex Media Server has at least one library with the type set to "Photos." You can add one in your Plex server's settings.

Can I use Phlix outside my home network?

Yes, as long as your Plex server allows remote access. Phlix connects to whichever server address is reachable — local or remote.

Does Phlix work with Plex Pass?

Phlix works with both free Plex accounts and Plex Pass. No Plex Pass is required.

Is Phlix made by Plex?

No. Phlix is an independent app not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Plex, Inc. It uses Plex's publicly available API.

How do I clear the image cache?

Go to Settings > Storage > Clear Image Cache. This frees up disk space without affecting your favorites or preferences.
