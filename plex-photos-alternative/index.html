A Plex Photos alternative for iPhone and iPad: Phlix

Updated November 24, 2026 · 4 min read

The short answer

Phlix is an independent iPhone and iPad app that works with Plex Media Server. It shows the photos and videos in your Plex photo libraries by folder or on a date timeline with a year scrubber, saves them to the Photos app or straight into an album, and plays slideshows of a folder (with the folders inside it), a year or the whole library.

With Screen Mirroring on, or over an HDMI adapter, the TV shows just the photos while your iPhone shows the controls (Phlix Pro; without Pro, the TV mirrors the phone). From version 1.0.21, Camera Upload (Beta) also backs up your camera roll to your Plex server at home.

Phlix is free to browse, so you can try it on your own library. Camera Upload needs Phlix 1.0.21 or later. If the App Store does not offer that version yet, it is on its way.

Plex Photos and Phlix

Plex Photos is Plex's own free app for photo libraries. Its App Store page lists three supported features: Recommended View, Timeline View and Library View. Its latest version is 2025.2.0, from June 12, 2025.

Phlix reads the same photo libraries on the same Plex Media Server. You do not have to choose: Phlix never changes or deletes what is on your server, so you can use it next to any Plex app. Here is what each one lists:

What Phlix does

  • Folders and a timeline. Folders that follow the folders on your server, and a date timeline one tap away. Tap a year on the scrubber, say 1987, to see just that year.
  • Built for big libraries. Phlix loads a page at a time instead of everything at once, so a library of 100,000 photos opens in seconds.
  • Save to Photos and Save to Album. Save photos and videos to the Photos app, one at a time: the original file when your server sends it, or a copy your server converted, which may be smaller. Pick an album once, and every save goes into it.
  • Slideshows. A folder's slideshow also plays the folders inside it (switch: Include Subfolders). 2, 4, 6 or 10 seconds a slide, shuffle and loop, with hard cuts between photos.
  • Big-screen slideshows. With Screen Mirroring on, or over an HDMI adapter, the TV shows just the photos while your iPhone shows the controls. A Phlix Pro feature; without Pro, the TV mirrors your phone.
  • Camera Upload (Beta). From version 1.0.21, Phlix backs up the photos and videos on your iPhone or iPad to a folder of your Plex photo library, on your home Wi-Fi only, through a free helper next to Plex Media Server. What happened to Plex's own Camera Upload, and how this one works.
  • Search and favorites. Search photo and video titles, tags and collections. Favorites are saved on your device.
  • Your server, or a shared one. Use your own server or one shared with you, and switch between its photo libraries.
  • Every screen. iPhone and iPad, with a sidebar of your libraries on iPad, VoiceOver support, and English and 15 more languages.
  • Careful with data. On cellular and in Low Data Mode, Phlix stops loading ahead of you.

What Phlix does not do

  • It plays photo libraries only: not your movie, TV or music libraries.
  • It does not edit photos, and it never changes or deletes what is on your server. Camera Upload only adds new files, in the folder you choose for it.
  • It does not convert files itself. HEIC, RAW and TIFF photos are converted by your Plex server; if it cannot convert one, Phlix shows the best copy it can get.
  • It is not on Android.

What it costs

Phlix is free to download and free to browse, with no ads. Browsing, the timeline, search, favorites and on-device slideshows are free forever, with 25 saves to Photos and, from version 1.0.21, the first 100 camera uploads. Phlix Pro removes those limits and adds big-screen slideshows on your TV: $19.99 once, with Family Sharing, or $6.99 a year. Try Pro free for 14 days: the trial is not a subscription, and it never charges you.

How to try it on your own library

  1. Download Phlix from the App Store.
  2. Sign in on Plex's own sign-in page. Phlix never sees your password, and there is no Phlix account to create.
  3. Pick your server and a photo library. Phlix needs a Plex Media Server with at least one photo library, and iOS 17 or later.

Questions

Is there an alternative to the Plex Photos app?

Yes. Phlix is an independent iPhone and iPad app that works with Plex Media Server. It shows the photos and videos in your Plex photo libraries by folder or on a date timeline with a year scrubber, saves them to the Photos app or into an album, and plays slideshows on the phone or on your TV. It is free to browse.

Is Phlix made by Plex?

No. Phlix is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed, or sponsored by Plex, Inc. It works with Plex Media Server.

Can I use Phlix and Plex Photos side by side?

Yes. Both read the same photo libraries on your Plex Media Server. Phlix never changes or deletes what is on your server, so you can keep using any Plex app next to it.

Do I need Plex Pass?

No Plex Pass is needed on your home network. For video, Plex requires a Plex Pass (the server owner's or your own) or a Remote Watch Pass to stream remotely; Plex says photos stream remotely for free (Plex: Requirements for Remote Playback of Personal Media). Some servers send only smaller copies of photos over the internet, so away from home a photo or a save can be a converted copy.

Does Phlix work with a Plex server that someone shared with me?

Yes. Use your own server or one shared with you, and switch between its photo libraries.

How much does Phlix cost?

Phlix is free to browse, with 25 free saves to Photos. Phlix Pro adds unlimited saves, big-screen slideshows on your TV and, from version 1.0.21, unlimited camera uploads: $19.99 once, with Family Sharing, or $6.99 a year. You can try Pro free for 14 days; the trial never renews and never charges you.

Can Phlix upload the photos on my iPhone to Plex?

Yes, from version 1.0.21: Camera Upload (Beta) copies the photos and videos on your iPhone or iPad into a folder of your Plex photo library, on your home Wi-Fi only, through a free helper you run in Docker next to Plex Media Server. If the App Store does not offer that version yet, it is on its way.

Does Phlix work on iPad and Mac?

Phlix is one app for iPhone and iPad; on iPad, a sidebar lists your libraries. On a Mac with Apple silicon, the App Store offers the iPad version. Apple lists it as designed for iPad and not verified for macOS.

Which languages does Phlix speak?

English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Dutch, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Polish, Japanese, Korean, and Chinese, Simplified and Traditional.
