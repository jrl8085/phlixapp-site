Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Overview

Phlix ("the App") is an independent, third-party photo and video browser that connects to a Plex Media Server that you own, operate, or are authorized to access. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, how it is processed, and what choices you have. By using the App you agree to the processing described below; if you do not agree, do not use the App.

The Short Version

Phlix collects anonymous product-interaction data (which features are used, where things break) to improve the app. This data is not linked to your identity and is not used for advertising or tracking. Phlix does not collect your name, email, photos, library contents, server name, server address, or Plex account information. You can opt out of analytics entirely in Settings → Privacy.

Anonymous Analytics Data We Collect

Phlix collects anonymous product-interaction data to understand which features are used, where users encounter problems, and how to improve the app. This data is sent to PostHog (US Cloud). The analytics implementation is a custom, dependency-free client — no third-party SDK is embedded in the app.

What is collected:

  • Which features are used (e.g. timeline viewed, slideshow started, search performed) and where errors occur (e.g. connection failed, image load failed).
  • App version, build number, iOS version, device model (e.g. "iPhone16,2"), and locale.
  • A randomly generated device identifier (UUID) that is not linked to your Apple ID, Plex account, or any personal information.
  • Purchase tier (free, trial, yearly, or lifetime) — but not transaction IDs, prices paid, or payment details.

What is never collected:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information.
  • Your Plex username, authentication tokens, server name, server address, or any server configuration.
  • Photos, videos, filenames, album names, or any content from your Plex library.
  • Apple advertising identifiers (IDFA), IP addresses logged by the developer, or cross-app tracking data.
  • Payment information, billing details, or transaction history.

You can opt out of analytics entirely in the App under Settings → Privacy → "Share anonymous usage data." When opted out, no data is sent and any queued data is deleted.

The App contains no advertising frameworks, no tracking pixels, and no session-replay tools. No ATT (App Tracking Transparency) prompt is required because no tracking occurs.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is stored on your device only and is never transmitted to the developer:

  • Plex Authentication Token — Stored securely in the iOS Keychain. Obtained directly from Plex, Inc. via Plex's official OAuth PIN flow. Used to authenticate API requests to your Plex Media Server.
  • Server Connection Details — The address(es), display name, and identifier of your Plex Media Server, stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • App Preferences — Selected library, slideshow defaults, mobile-data preferences, and similar settings, stored in UserDefaults on your device.
  • Favorites — A list of media-item identifiers you have favorited within the App, stored in a local file on your device.
  • Image and Metadata Cache — Thumbnails, full-resolution images, and library indexes are cached on your device to improve performance. You can clear the image cache at any time in the App's Settings, or by uninstalling the App.

This information is deleted from your device when you uninstall the App. It is not backed up or transmitted to the developer.

Network Communication

While running, the App communicates directly with the following third parties. The developer is not party to and does not intercept these communications.

  • Plex, Inc. (plex.tv) — Used for authentication via Plex's OAuth PIN flow and for discovering Plex Media Servers associated with your Plex account. Communication is between your device and Plex's servers. Plex's handling of your data is governed by Plex's Privacy Policy.
  • Your Plex Media Server — Used to browse, search, stream, and download media stored on that server. Communication is between your device and the server's address (which may be on your local network or reached over the internet, depending on how you configure your Plex Media Server).
  • Apple, Inc. — The App is distributed through the Apple App Store; Apple processes purchases and subscriptions, distributes the App, and may collect data about App downloads and use in accordance with Apple's standard policies. Apple's handling of that data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive your name, email address, billing information, or any other identifying purchase data from Apple.
  • PostHog, Inc. (us.posthog.com) — Anonymous product-interaction events are sent to PostHog's US Cloud infrastructure for analytics. No personal data, Plex credentials, server addresses, or media content is included. See "Anonymous Analytics Data We Collect" above for the full list. You can opt out in the App's Settings. PostHog's handling of data is governed by PostHog's Privacy Policy.

Photo Library Access

If you choose to save downloaded media to your device's Photo Library, the App will request the iOS "Add Photos Only" permission. That permission is used solely to save media you explicitly choose to download. The App does not request, read, or access your existing Photo Library content.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App does not collect personal information from anyone, no special procedures are required for users of any age. Parents and guardians should be aware that the App requires a Plex account and access to a Plex Media Server, and should ensure that content accessible via that server is appropriate for any child using the App.

Your Choices and Your Rights

Because the developer does not collect or store any personal information about you, there is nothing for the developer to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. To exercise the equivalent of access, correction, deletion, or portability rights with respect to data stored on your device:

  • Delete all local data: Uninstall the App. iOS will remove all of the App's local storage, including the Keychain entries and image caches it created.
  • Clear caches without uninstalling: Open the App, then Settings, and use the cache-clearing controls.
  • Log out: The App's logout flow removes Plex authentication tokens and server connection details from the Keychain.

Data stored on your own Plex Media Server is under your control, not the developer's. Data Plex, Inc. and Apple, Inc. hold about you is governed by their respective policies linked above; please contact them directly to exercise any rights with respect to that data.

California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

The developer does not "sell" or "share" your personal information as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act. The anonymous analytics data collected by the App is not linked to any individual and does not constitute personal information under the CCPA/CPRA.

EEA / UK Residents (GDPR / UK GDPR)

The anonymous analytics data collected by the App uses a randomly generated device identifier that cannot be linked to an identifiable person. Where Plex, Inc., Apple, Inc., or PostHog, Inc. acts as a data controller or processor in connection with your use of their services, their respective privacy policies and data-controller obligations apply. You can opt out of analytics at any time in Settings.

Security

The App uses the iOS Keychain to store sensitive credentials (Plex authentication tokens and server connection details). Network requests use HTTPS where supported by your Plex Media Server. No transmission over the internet or storage on any device can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and you assume the residual risk of using the App.
