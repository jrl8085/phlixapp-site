Frequently Asked Questions

What is Phlix?

Phlix is an iOS photo browser for Plex Media Server. It displays your photos in a chronological timeline with a year scrubber, saves any photo or clip to the Photos app, and runs big-screen slideshows: turn on Screen Mirroring (or plug in an HDMI adapter) and Phlix takes over the TV with just the photo on the TV and the controls on your phone. Built for libraries of 100,000+ photos.

Do I need Plex Pass to use Phlix?

No Plex Pass is needed on your home network. Away from home, Plex requires the server owner to have a Plex Pass (or you a Remote Watch Pass) to stream personal video — including clips in Phlix — and Plex's photo exemption is stated only for its own Plex Photos app, so photos may be affected too. Phlix itself needs only a Plex account and a Plex Media Server with at least one photo library.

What makes Phlix different from the official Plex Photos app?

Phlix was built from scratch to be the photo experience Plex users need. It offers a chronological timeline with a year scrubber, handles 100,000+ photo libraries, runs big-screen slideshows with Screen Mirroring or HDMI (just the photo on the TV, the controls on your phone), saves photos to the Photos app, includes an automatic cellular data saver, and receives frequent developer updates, while the official Plex Photos app has not been updated since June 2025.

How many photos can Phlix handle?

Phlix is built for scale. It uses server-side year filtering, virtual paging, and intelligent prefetching to handle libraries of 100,000+ photos. The first screen appears in seconds; the year index fills in behind it. If you have an enormous family photo collection, Phlix was built for you.

How does the AirPlay slideshow work?

Turn on Screen Mirroring (or plug in an HDMI adapter) and Phlix takes over the TV: just the photo on the TV, the controls on your phone — not a copy of your phone screen. Phlix shows photos at the resolution your display reports to iOS — up to 4K over a USB-C HDMI adapter. Taking over the TV is a Phlix Pro feature; without Pro, the TV simply mirrors your phone.

Is my data private with Phlix?

Yes. Phlix has no ads, no tracking pixels, and no session replay. It collects only anonymous usage data (which features are used, where errors occur) to improve the app. No photos, filenames, server addresses, Plex credentials, names or email addresses are ever collected. You can opt out entirely in Settings. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I use Phlix outside my home network?

Yes, as long as your Plex server allows remote access. Phlix connects to whichever server address is reachable, local or remote. Note that Plex may require the server owner to have a Plex Pass (or you a Remote Watch Pass) before it serves files remotely to any app, including Phlix.

Does the lifetime purchase include Family Sharing?

Yes. The $19.99 Pro Lifetime purchase is Family Shareable through Apple Family Sharing. Buy once and your whole family can use Pro on their devices.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

After 14 days, Pro features (unlimited saves to Photos, big-screen slideshows) lock and Phlix returns to the free tier. You keep full access to browsing, timeline, search, favorites, and on-device slideshows. Nothing is deleted and nothing is charged. You can upgrade to Pro anytime.

Is Phlix made by Plex?

No. Phlix is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed, or sponsored by Plex, Inc. It connects to your Plex Media Server using publicly available APIs.
