Frequently Asked Questions

What is Phlix?

Phlix is an iOS photo browser for Plex Media Server. It displays your photos in a chronological timeline with a year scrubber, supports 4K slideshows on your TV via AirPlay 2 extended display, and lets you download photos to your iOS Photo Library for offline access. Built for libraries of 150,000+ photos.

Do I need Plex Pass to use Phlix?

No. Phlix works with both free Plex accounts and Plex Pass. All you need is a Plex Media Server with at least one photo library. No Plex Pass required.

What makes Phlix different from the official Plex Photos app?

Phlix was built from scratch to be the photo experience Plex users need. It offers a chronological timeline with year scrubber, handles 150K+ photo libraries smoothly, supports 4K slideshows on external displays via AirPlay 2, provides offline downloads to your iOS Photos, includes automatic cellular data saver, and receives weekly developer updates. These are features the official Plex Photos app doesn't offer.

How many photos can Phlix handle?

Phlix is engineered for scale. It uses server-side year filtering, virtual paging, and intelligent prefetching to handle libraries of 150,000+ photos with smooth scrolling and fast load times. If you have an enormous family photo collection, Phlix was built for you.

How does the AirPlay slideshow work?

Connect to an AirPlay 2 extended display or plug in an HDMI adapter. Phlix renders photos at full 4K resolution on your TV while your iPhone stays as the controller with playback controls. This is a true dual-screen experience, not screen mirroring. Phlix also supports AirPlay Screen Mirroring for any AirPlay-compatible TV.

Is my data private with Phlix?

Yes. Phlix has no ads, no tracking pixels, and no session replay. It collects only anonymous usage data (which features are used, where errors occur) to improve the app. No photos, filenames, server addresses, Plex credentials, or personal information are ever collected. You can opt out entirely in Settings. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I use Phlix outside my home network?

Yes, as long as your Plex server allows remote access. Phlix connects to whichever server address is reachable, local or remote.

Does the lifetime purchase include Family Sharing?

Yes. The $19.99 Pro Lifetime purchase is Family Shareable through Apple Family Sharing. Buy once and your whole family can use Pro on their devices.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

After 14 days, Pro features (unlimited downloads, external display slideshows) lock and Phlix returns to the free tier. You keep full access to browsing, timeline, search, favorites, and on-device slideshows. Nothing is deleted and nothing is charged. You can upgrade to Pro anytime.

Is Phlix made by Plex?

No. Phlix is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed, or sponsored by Plex, Inc. It connects to your Plex Media Server using publicly available APIs.
