Become a beta tester

Before a new version of Phlix reaches the App Store, a small group of testers can try it. Testers get beta versions through TestFlight, Apple's app for beta versions, and tell the developer what works and what doesn't.

Ask to join with the form below. It is free, and you can leave at any time.

How it works

  1. Send the form. Your request goes to the developer, who reads every one.
  2. Apple sends the invitation. When you are added and a beta version is ready, TestFlight emails an invitation to the address you gave. It comes from Apple, and it can take a few days: testers are added a few at a time.
  3. Install the beta. Get the free TestFlight app from the App Store, open the invitation on your iPhone or iPad, and install Phlix from TestFlight. New beta versions then arrive there.

Good to know

  • Camera Upload needs no beta. Camera Upload (Beta) is already in the App Store version of Phlix, 1.0.21 or later. TestFlight is only for trying new versions before they are released.
  • What you need. An iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later, and a Plex Media Server with a photo library. You must be 13 or older: that is Apple's rule for TestFlight. Phlix is an independent app that works with Plex Media Server.
  • Betas are unfinished. Something may not work. Keep your own backup of anything you cannot replace, as you would anyway.
  • Your photos are not touched. Phlix never changes or deletes what is on your server, in a beta as in the App Store version. A beta installs over the App Store version and keeps your settings and favorites, which are stored on your device. To go back, choose Stop Testing in TestFlight and install Phlix from the App Store again. If you have to delete the beta first, its settings and favorites go with it.
  • Your Phlix Pro, in a beta. A beta uses Apple's test store and does not see what you bought on the App Store. If you own Phlix Pro or are in the 14-day trial, the beta shows the free version until you choose Pro again inside the beta. Apple does not charge for that, and nothing chosen in a beta carries over. Choose the one-time purchase there: a yearly plan bought in a beta ends after a short time. Your real purchase is there again when you go back to the App Store version.
  • Each beta expires. A beta version stops opening 90 days after it was made; TestFlight shows the days left. Install the next beta, or the App Store version, before then.
  • Telling us what you find. In a beta, Send Feedback in Phlix's Settings reaches the developer, and so does a screenshot shared through TestFlight.

What happens with your details

This page loads Web3Forms and hCaptcha when you open it, so both see your IP address and browser details; hCaptcha checks that you are a person. The form sends your name, email address and answers, with your IP address, to the developer by email through Web3Forms, the same service as the support form. Web3Forms also keeps a copy of what you send on its servers.

To invite you, the developer enters your name and email address in Apple's TestFlight. While you use a beta, Apple collects the following automatically and shows it to the developer next to your name and email address, and you cannot switch it off: whether you accepted the invitation and installed a beta, which version, your device model, iOS version and language, how many times you opened the beta, how many times it crashed, and the crash logs. Feedback you choose to send through TestFlight, such as a screenshot or a comment, reaches the developer with a few more details of the device, such as its time zone, carrier, battery level and free storage. See Apple's TestFlight & Privacy.

A beta also sends the same anonymous usage data as the App Store version, marked as coming from a beta, unless you turn it off in Phlix's Settings. With few testers, the device model and iOS version that TestFlight shows next to your name can be matched to those records, and the developer may look at them to investigate what you report.

Your details are used for the beta program only. To leave, choose Stop Testing in TestFlight. To have your request and details deleted, at Web3Forms and in TestFlight too, write through the support page. More in the privacy policy.
